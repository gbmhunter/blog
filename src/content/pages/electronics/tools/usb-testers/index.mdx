## FNIRSI USB Testers

FNIRSI manufactures a range of inline USB testers with their own screens for reporting things like voltages, currents and power.

**FNB38**

The FNB38 is an exposed PCB sandwich style inline USB tester with a 1.44" TFT display. Can measure voltages from 4–24 V and currents from 0–5 A. Max. power is 120 W. As of November 2026 the FNB38 is no longer available on the FNIRSI website, only the upgraded FNB58.

<Image src={import('./_assets/fnirsi-fnb38-usb-tester-photo.webp')} width="400px">A photo of the FNIRSI FNB38 USB tester.[^kunkune-fnirsi-fnb38]</Image>

**FNB58**

The FNB58 is an upgraded version of the FNB38. It has an enclosure and a 2" TFT display (bigger than the FNB38 1.44" display). It has a male USB Type A for the logging/control port. Has Type C and Micro female connectors for the monitor IN, and Type C for the monitor OUT. Supports QC2.0/QC3.0, FCP/SCP, AFC, PD2.0/3.0, VOOC/WARP, and Super VOOC 1.0/2.0. It supports monitor voltages from 4–28 V and currents from 0–7 A. Supports a max. power of 140 W.[^fnirsi-fnb58-usb-fast-charge-tester]
