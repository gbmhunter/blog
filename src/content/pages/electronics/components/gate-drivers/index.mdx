A _gate driver_ is an IC that sits between a logic-level control signal (e.g. a PWM output from a microcontroller) and the gate of a power switch such as a [MOSFET](/electronics/components/transistors/mosfets/), [IGBT](/electronics/components/transistors/insulated-gate-bipolar-transistors-igbts/) or GaN/SiC transistor. It provides the large peak currents needed to charge and discharge the gate capacitance quickly, and (depending on the topology) shifts the drive voltage up to a floating high-side source node (or isolates it from the control ground altogether).

## Topologies

### Low-side Gate Drivers

The drivers will typically accept a wide range of voltage for the `VDD` pin (the pin the gate will be driven to when going HIGH). They will decouple the logic-level signals from this such that a 3.3V logic-level input will still be considered HIGH when `VDD` is say 12V.

## Part Recommendations

### Texas Instruments UCC2751x

The Texas Instruments UCC2751x is a family of single-channel, high-speed, low-side gate drivers. It is capable of 4 A peak when both sourcing and sinking current to the gate. They are available in SON-6 and SOT-23-5 packages.[^ti-ucc27517-datasheet]

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| VDD | 4.5V to 18V |
| Ipeak source/sink | 4A/4A or 4A/8A |
| Propagation delay | 13 ns (typ.) |

<Image src={import('./_assets/ti-ucc2751x-gate-driver-typical-application-diagram.webp')} width="700px">A typical application diagram for the Texas Instruments UCC2751x gate driver family. Note how you can use it in a inverting or non-inverting configuration depending on what pin you wire your logic-level control signal to.[^ti-ucc27517-datasheet]</Image>

`VDD` can be anywhere from 4.5V to 18V, and the logic inputs support both TTL and CMOS levels. The propagation delay is typically 13 ns.
