The _PowerPAK 1212-8_ is a small leadless power package from Vishay, commonly used for MOSFETs. It comes in Single and Dual variants.

It uses the same packaging technology as the larger [PowerPAK SO-8 package](/pcb-design/component-packages/powerpak-so8-component-package/).[^vishay-si7101dn-ds]

## Variants

- PowerPAK 1212-8W Single: The `W` stands for "wettable" flanks, which aids optical inspection especially in automotive applications.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]

## PowerPAK 1212-8 Single

This uses the industry standard pinout of pins 1-3 for the source, pin 4 for the gate, and pins 5-8 for the drain.

<Image src={import('./_assets/powerpak-1212-8-single-photo-top-and-bottom-with-mosfet-pinout.webp')} width="600px">Top and bottom views of the PowerPAK 1212-8 Single package, showing the MOSFET pinout. The drain pins 5-8 are connected to the large exposed pad on the underside.[^vishay-si7101dn-ds]</Image>

### Thermal Resistance

Junction to case thermal resistance is quoted at $R_{\theta JC} = 2.4^\circ C/W$.[^vishay-si7101dn-ds]

### Dimensions

The Single and Dual variants share the same package outline, and only differ in the thermal pad(s) on the underside. The body is 3.30 x 3.30 mm (nominal) with a height of 1.04 mm (nominal) and a pin pitch of 0.65 mm.[^vishay-sisa14dn-ds]

<Image src={import('./_assets/powerpak-1212-8-single-dual-dimensions.webp')} width="800px">Package dimensions for the PowerPAK 1212-8, covering both the Single and Dual variants.[^vishay-sisa14dn-ds]</Image>

### Recommended Footprint

The image below shows Vishay's recommended minimum pads for the PowerPAK 1212-8 Single. Dimensions are in inches, with millimetres in brackets.

<Image src={import('./_assets/powerpak-1212-8-single-recommended-footprint.webp')} width="600px">Recommended minimum pads (footprint) for the PowerPAK 1212-8 Single package.[^vishay-si7101dn-ds]</Image>

## PowerPAK 1212-8 Dual

### Thermal Resistance

Junction to case thermal resistance is quoted at $R_{\theta JC} = 5.5^\circ C/W$.[^vishay-si7101dn-ds]

### Dimensions

See the dimensions above, as they share the same outline except for the thermal pad(s) on the underside.

## PowerPAK 1212-8W Single

The PowerPAK 1212-8W Single is the same as the standard Single variant, except the flanks of the package are "wettable" to aid optical inspection. This is especially useful in automotive applications.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]

<Image src={import('./_assets/powerpak-1212-8w-single-photo-top-and-bottom.webp')} width="600px">Top and bottom views of the PowerPAK 1212-8W Single package, showing the MOSFET pinout. Note the notches in the ends of the pins, which form the wettable flanks.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]</Image>

### Dimensions

The dimensions are shown below and are the same as the standard PowerPAK 1212-8, except for an additional A2 dimension (0–0.13 mm) on the pins.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]

<Image src={import('./_assets/powerpak-1212-8w-dimensions.webp')} width="800px">Package dimensions for the PowerPAK 1212-8W.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]</Image>

### Recommended Footprint

The recommended minimum pads for the PowerPAK 1212-8W Single are exactly the same as the standard Single variant as shown above.[^vishay-sq7414cenw-ds]
